How to use Framer — step-by-step guide

Framer turns a design into a live, hosted website without code. This is the path Pakistani freelancers use to deliver client sites in days, not weeks. If you have not activated yet, the plans, prices and activation details are on the Framer product page.

  • Step 1 — Start from a template or AI prompt. Choose a quality template close to your goal, or describe the site to Framer’s AI builder and refine the result. Structure first, decoration later.
  • Step 2 — Customise with your real content. Replace text, photos and colours with the actual business content. Set your fonts and brand colours in Site Styles so the whole site stays consistent.
  • Step 3 — Add pages and a CMS. Build the core pages (Home, Services, Contact) and use the CMS for repeatable content like blog posts, products or portfolio items.
  • Step 4 — Connect your custom domain. A paid plan lets you publish on your own domain with SSL included — essential for a business site that takes enquiries seriously.
  • Step 5 — Add forms and publish. Connect the contact/WhatsApp enquiry form, check the mobile layout carefully (most of your visitors are on phones), then publish.

Framer tips and best practices

  • Design mobile-first in Framer’s breakpoints — over 80% of Pakistani traffic is mobile.
  • Keep animations light on the hero; heavy effects slow the first screen on 4G.
  • Use the CMS from day one for anything you will update monthly — rebuilding static pages gets old fast.
  • Coming from Figma? The Figma-to-Framer copy workflow is the fastest way to rebuild an existing design.

Frequently asked questions

Can I connect my own domain?
Yes — the Basic plan includes a custom domain with free SSL.
Do I need coding skills?
No. Framer is fully visual; the AI generates the starting design for you.
How many sites can I publish?
Plan limits apply per site — details are shared on WhatsApp before you order.
Is hosting included?
Yes — fast global hosting is built in, no separate hosting bill.
Is there a warranty?
Yes — replacement warranty included.
How do I publish a Framer website on my own domain?
On a paid plan, add your domain in Site Settings, update the DNS records Framer shows you at your registrar, and wait for the SSL certificate to activate automatically. Your site then runs on your domain with hosting handled by Framer.
Can Framer handle a business website with a blog?
Yes. Framer includes a CMS for blogs, portfolios and product listings, plus forms and basic SEO settings per page. It suits business and portfolio sites very well, while very large online stores are better on a dedicated e-commerce platform.
