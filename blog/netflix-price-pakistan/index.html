How to use Netflix — step-by-step guide

A Netflix profile set up properly in the first five minutes saves months of small annoyances. Here is the setup regular Pakistani viewers use. If you have not activated yet, the plans, prices and activation details are on the Netflix product page.

  • Step 1 — Sign in and pick your own profile. Always watch inside your assigned profile so your watchlist, progress and recommendations stay separate from everyone else’s.
  • Step 2 — Set your languages. Choose English or Urdu audio/subtitle preferences in profile settings. Many popular shows and movies offer Urdu subtitles or dubbing.
  • Step 3 — Build your list first. Add shows to “My List” whenever you hear a recommendation — deciding what to watch becomes a 10-second job later.
  • Step 4 — Download for travel and load-shedding. On mobile, download episodes over Wi-Fi in advance. Downloads keep working offline, which is ideal for travel and power cuts.
  • Step 5 — Use search codes and categories. Search by genre (“Korean thriller”, “Pakistani drama”) and check the Top 10 row to find what is trending in Pakistan this week.
