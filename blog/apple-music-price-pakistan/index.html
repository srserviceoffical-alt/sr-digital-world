How to use Apple Music — step-by-step guide

Apple Music is built around sound quality and a properly organised library. This quick setup gets both right on iPhone and Android. If you have not activated yet, the plans, prices and activation details are on the Apple Music product page.

  • Step 1 — Turn on Sync Library. Enable it in settings so your songs, playlists and likes appear on every device signed in with your Apple ID.
  • Step 2 — Set audio quality to Lossless. In settings choose Lossless (and Dolby Atmos where available). On mobile data, keep streaming at High to save your package.
  • Step 3 — Build your library, not just playlists. Add albums and songs to your library — Apple Music’s recommendations and your personal mixes are built from what you add and play.
  • Step 4 — Download your core playlists. Download your gym, drive and focus playlists for offline listening, especially before travel.
  • Step 5 — Explore Pakistani and regional music. Search for your favourite Pakistani, Punjabi, Urdu and Bollywood artists — the catalogue covers them alongside the full international library of 100 million+ songs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get Apple Music access?
You receive a private account login. Sign in to the Apple Music app on any device.
Does it include lossless audio?
Yes — full lossless and Dolby Atmos quality is included wherever your device supports it.
Can I use it on Android too?
Yes. Apple Music works on iPhone, Android, Windows and the web with the same account.
What about the free trial?
The official one-month trial applies to new direct subscriptions; the 5-month plan is already discounted below the official monthly rate.
Is my listening private?
Yes — the login is private to you; your library and playlists are your own.
How do I get the best sound quality on Apple Music?
Turn on Lossless Audio in settings and enable Dolby Atmos for supported tracks. Use wired earphones or good Bluetooth headphones to hear the difference, and download in lossless over Wi-Fi because the files are much larger than standard quality.
Does Apple Music work on Android phones?
Yes. The official Apple Music app on Android offers the same 100 million+ song catalogue, lossless audio and downloads. Your library syncs through your Apple ID, so you can listen on Android, iPhone, Windows and the web.
