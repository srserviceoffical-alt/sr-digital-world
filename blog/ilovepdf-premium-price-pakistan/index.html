How to use iLovePDF Premium — step-by-step guide

iLovePDF Premium is a time-saver for anyone who handles documents daily. These are the workflows Pakistani offices, students and freelancers use most. If you have not activated yet, the plans, prices and activation details are on the iLovePDF Premium product page.

  • Step 1 — Merge and organise first. Combine scattered scans and pages into one PDF, reorder pages, and delete blanks. One clean file is easier to compress, sign and send.
  • Step 2 — Compress before sharing. Use compression before sending files on WhatsApp or email — Premium compression keeps text sharp while cutting file size dramatically.
  • Step 3 — Convert with OCR. Convert scanned pages into editable Word or Excel files. OCR recognises printed Urdu and English text, turning paper forms into documents you can actually edit.

iLovePDF Premium tips and best practices

  • Always keep the original file before compressing or converting — work on a copy.
  • For official submissions (university, embassy, bank), use ‘Extreme’ compression only if the portal has a small size limit; otherwise choose the recommended level for readability.
  • The desktop app works offline — better for sensitive documents you do not want to upload.
  • Name output files clearly (Name_Document_Date.pdf); offices reject messy files more often than large ones.

How do I convert a scanned document into editable Word?
Upload the scan to the PDF-to-Word tool with OCR enabled. iLovePDF reads the printed text — English and Urdu — and rebuilds it as an editable document. Check numbers and names after conversion, as handwriting and blurry scans can produce small errors.
Is it safe to upload documents to iLovePDF?
Files are processed over an encrypted connection and automatically deleted from servers after processing. For highly sensitive documents, use the iLovePDF desktop app instead, which converts files locally on your own computer without uploading them.
